PVR INOX · take‑private thesis

A sponsor‑side LBO of India’s largest multiplex chain, built from a Blackstone lens. Entry and exit multiple analysis, debt paydown mechanics, and full sensitivity tables. The model below is live: drag the slider, hover the grid.

The thesis rested on post‑merger screen consolidation, FOCO‑model capex relief, and advertising revenue recovery. D&A treatment under Ind AS 116 materially distorts headline EBITDA, so the model works from pre‑lease EBITDA and treats lease liabilities as debt‑like.

A companion DCF (12.3% WACC, 4% terminal growth) triangulated a blended target of ₹1,200–1,425 per share, which frames the exit multiple assumptions in the engine on the right.

M&A · Valuation Independent · 2025

Zomato × Blinkit · the quick‑commerce bet

Did the ₹4,447 cr all‑stock acquisition create or destroy value? A sum‑of‑the‑parts DCF and a value bridge that decomposes where the market re‑rated the combined entity.

The market initially priced Blinkit as a cash incinerator. The bridge tracks the story the numbers ended up telling: dark‑store contribution margins turning positive, AOV expansion, and the ad‑monetisation layer that food delivery had already proven.

The exercise sharpened a habit I have kept since: separate the deal you can defend on paper from the option value you are actually underwriting.

Quant · Research Independent · 2026

Survivorship bias in search fund returns

Reported search fund IRRs are computed on the funds that lived to report. This study simulates the full cohort, failures included, using a Heston stochastic‑volatility core with Merton jump‑diffusion shocks, and measures how much the survivor‑only lens inflates the story.

Each simulated fund follows enterprise value under Heston dynamics, where volatility itself is stochastic and mean‑reverting, with Merton jumps standing in for the discrete shocks that actually kill small companies: a lost anchor client, a key‑person exit, a regulatory hit. Paths that breach a distress barrier are absorbed as failures.

The headline result is the gap between the two medians below. Averaging only the survivors is not a small rounding error; it is a different asset class. The exhibit re‑simulates a fresh cohort in your browser each time you run it.
